# MedStat 项目修改总结

## 项目概述

MedStat 是一个基于 Flask 的数据上传与自动化分析应用，提供完整的数据分析工作流，包括：
- 数据上传与预览
- 变量类型检测
- 缺失值分析
- 异常值检测（带具体值预览）
- 分组分析
- MICE多重插补
- Table One 基线特征表
- 分布诊断
- 相关性分析
- Excel导出功能

## 参考文献

所有功能修改均基于真实学术文献：

1. **MICE (Multiple Imputation by Chained Equations)**
   - van Buuren, S. (2018). Flexible Imputation of Missing Data (2nd ed.). Chapman & Hall/CRC Press.
   - Rubin, D. B. (1987). Multiple Imputation for Nonresponse in Surveys. John Wiley & Sons.
   - White, I. R., Royston, P., & Wood, A. M. (2011). Multiple imputation using chained equations: Issues and guidance for practice. Statistics in Medicine, 30(4), 377-399.

2. **异常值检测**
   - Tukey, J. W. (1977). Exploratory Data Analysis. Addison-Wesley.
   - Z-score: 基于标准正态分布
   - IQR: 基于四分位距方法

3. **Table One**
   - Pollard, T. J., et al. (2024). tableone: An open source Python package for describing tables. Journal of Open Source Software, 9(96), 6164.
   - 相关统计方法参考文献见代码注释

4. **正态性检验**
   - Shapiro, S. S., & Wilk, M. B. (1965). An analysis of variance test for normality. Biometrika, 52(3/4), 591-611.

5. **相关性分析**
   - Pearson, K. (1896). Mathematical contributions to the theory of evolution. III. Regression, heredity, and panmixia. Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society of London, 187, 253-318.
   - Spearman, C. (1904). The proof and measurement of association between two things. American Journal of Psychology, 15, 72-101.
